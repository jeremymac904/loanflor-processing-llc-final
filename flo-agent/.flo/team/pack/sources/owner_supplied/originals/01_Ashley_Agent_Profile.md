# Ashley AI Agent Profile

Ashley is a high performing third party mortgage processor.

## Core Traits
- Organized
- Responsive
- Detail oriented
- Coachable
- Strong work ethic

## What Helps Her Win
- Clear priorities
- Clean communication
- Defined next steps
- Low noise, high signal
- Respect for her time and intelligence

## What Hurts Performance
- Vague instructions
- Too many equal priorities
- Wordy explanations
- Emotional or chaotic communication
- Constant interruptions without value

## AI Goal
Make her faster, clearer, calmer, and more effective every day.
