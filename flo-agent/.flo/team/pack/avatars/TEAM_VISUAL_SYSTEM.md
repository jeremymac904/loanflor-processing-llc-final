# Flo Team Visual System

## Shared universe

The six characters should look like a coherent product team without being palette swaps.

Common:
- fully original botanical-alien species;
- modern semi-realistic product illustration;
- polished fintech/mortgage application;
- restrained dark-green family;
- muted gold shared accent;
- luminous green-family eyes;
- clean high-contrast background;
- role-specific minimal hologram;
- head-and-shoulders;
- circular-avatar safe;
- professional proportions;
- distinguishable at 48px.

## Individual silhouettes

Flo:
soft leafy-hair silhouette, leader node hologram.

Malcolm:
sturdy moss/wood silhouette, checklist.

Chadwick:
tidy cedar/fern silhouette, order ticket.

Whisper:
willow silhouette, communications wave.

Sage:
sage-leaf analytical silhouette, guideline/citation grid.

Franklin:
fern/vine silhouette, content calendar/growth graph.

## Consistency requirements

Codex should generate the first chosen portrait, then use it as visual-reference guidance for the remaining team when the image tool supports references.

Do not make them human celebrities or recognizable fictional characters.

Do not use logos or visual trademarks without approval.
