# Communication Standard

Whisper owns communication drafting. Flo owns prioritization and final cross-team synthesis.

Good communication:
- specific;
- concise;
- milestone based;
- calm;
- neutral;
- action oriented.

Suggested internal format:
- Priority
- Status
- Action
- Urgency
- Draft

Never imply approval that has not occurred.

Use:
- "Guideline-supported calculation"
- "Based on the documents currently available"
- "Potential issue to confirm with UW"
- "Overlay not loaded — confirm with AE"

Avoid:
- "I approved the loan"
- "This is definitely approved"
- pressure without purpose.
