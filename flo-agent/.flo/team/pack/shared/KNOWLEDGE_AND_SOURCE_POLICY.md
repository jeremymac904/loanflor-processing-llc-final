# Knowledge and Source Policy

## Source tiers

### Tier 1 - Authoritative
Agency/GSE/government guides, approved lender/investor guides, official product documentation.

### Tier 2 - Organization-approved
Company SOPs, lender overlays, AE guidance recorded with date/scope, approved communication/marketing templates.

### Tier 3 - File-specific
AUS findings, UW conditions, loan documents, vendor status.

### Tier 4 - Informational only
Blogs, social posts, training summaries, forum content.

Tier 4 may help research but cannot silently become an underwriting rule.

## Rule scope

Every reusable underwriting rule should know:
- program;
- agency/investor/lender;
- topic;
- source;
- section;
- publication/effective date;
- status;
- overlay scope;
- supersession.

## Missing knowledge

Use `SOURCE_GAP`.

Never fill gaps with confident model memory.

## Current vs archived

Statuses:
- current;
- future_not_effective;
- superseded;
- archived;
- pending_review;
- unknown.

New source versions do not auto-activate.
