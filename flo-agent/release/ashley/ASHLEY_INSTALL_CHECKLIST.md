# Ashley's Flo Setup — 7 Steps

## Before you start
1. **Docker Desktop** — download from [docker.com](https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop/) and install. Restart your PC if prompted. Docker is required for Flo to sign documents locally.
2. **Ollama** — download from [ollama.com](https://ollama.com) and install. Ollama lets Flo answer mortgage questions on your own PC, without the internet.

## Install Flo
3. **Run `Flo-0.17.0-win-x64-portable.zip`** — double-click to open it, then drag `Flo.exe` to your Desktop (or any folder you like). Double-click `Flo.exe` to launch.

   > If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## Connect your accounts
4. **Connect Google** — click **Connect Google** on the first screen. A browser window will open — sign into your Google account, click **Allow**, copy the code Flo shows, and paste it back. This lets Flo access your Drive files and Calendar.

5. **Connect Gmail** — click **Connect Gmail**, enter your Gmail address and the App Password you generated. Click **Save**. Flo reads lender emails automatically.

6. **Connect Zapier** *(optional)* — click **Connect Zapier**, paste the webhook URL from your Zap, click **Save**.

7. **Set Up Local Signing** — click **Set Up Local Signing**. If Docker isn't running yet, Flo will tell you — start Docker Desktop first, then click again. When you see **Ready**, you're done.

8. **Set Up Local AI** — click **Set Up Local AI**. Ollama will download a small model. This takes a few minutes on first run. When you see **Ready**, you're done.

## Enter Flo
9. Click **Continue**. You're in Flo.
